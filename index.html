重/解構 re(li)de(bra)structure(ry)

Seeing is not always believing.

In everyday life, objects seem to carry fixed functions and forms — a chair is for sitting, a bookshelf is for holding books, and everything appears self-evident. But what happens when we take these familiar objects apart and put them back together differently? Might we discover that their essence and purpose hold more possibilities than we assumed? This exhibition invites you into a library turned upside down — one where chairs and bookshelves no longer follow convention, and where you'll find books that are fictional, absurd, or entirely impossible to realize.

By unsettling our understanding of what a "normal" object should look like, this exhibition hopes to spark a new way of imagining the familiar. You may find yourself asking: are the things we consider "normal" actually fact — or just a shared illusion we've agreed to believe? This exhibition invites you to move between truth and fallacy, challenging your past standards and judgments, and breaking down the bias against the "abnormal."

Part of the 9th NCCU Arts Festival — "Co-Weird Paradox," May 2025.

Co-curated with Violette Chen.
