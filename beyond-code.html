A bit of a side story: a few other sides of me, outside of engineering.

Beyond Code
Portrait

I love photographing people, especially the in-between moments, right before or after a pose, when they forget the camera's even there. Those shots are always the best ones.

Out and About

I really love to travel and shoot street photography, just wandering around and snapping whatever catches my eye. These three are from Okinawa, UCLA, and Yosemite.

Film

I like not knowing what I got until the roll's developed. It makes me actually slow down and think about the shot instead of just spamming the shutter.

Oil Painting

This is where I slow down the most. I especially love painting portraits. It forces me to really study a face instead of just glancing at it, catching all the small asymmetries and expressions a photo would blur past.

Curation

重/解構 re(li)de(bra)structure(ry)

We basically took everyday objects, like chairs and bookshelves, and rebuilt them into weird, impossible versions, just to mess with what people assume is "normal."

Part of the 9th NCCU Arts Festival, "Co-Weird Paradox," May 2025.

Co-curated with Violette Chen.
